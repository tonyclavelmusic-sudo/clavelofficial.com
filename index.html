Bio

CLAVEL is a house and tech house DJ and producer from Orange County, California. The name is his father’s band — Los Claveles Rojos — and the rhythm came with it: he grew up playing drums, alongside the shaker, cowbell and claves his father kept around the house.

Self-taught as a producer and sharpened through Repopulate Mars coursework led by Lee Foss, John Summit and Detlef, he engineers and mixes every record he puts out. That work put him on Obsessions Recordings in March 2026, with “BBB” landing on the label’s Miami Music Week compilation, Ritual(s) Vol. 2. Behind the decks he’s held residencies across Orange County and played out to San Francisco and El Paso, Texas.
