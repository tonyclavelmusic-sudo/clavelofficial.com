Privacy

Last updated 20 October 2026

This site is run by CLAVEL, a DJ and producer based in Orange County, California. This page says what the site collects, why, and who else receives it. Questions about it: info@clavelofficial.com.

What this site collects

Nothing directly. There are no forms, accounts, newsletter sign-ups or analytics, and the site doesn’t set cookies or store anything on your device.

If you email us, we receive your message and your email address and use them only to reply — for example, about a booking.

Who else receives information

Visiting any website sends some technical information — your IP address, browser type and the page requested — to the companies that deliver it:

  • GitHub hosts this site (GitHub Pages) and keeps server logs of every request, including your IP address. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • SoundCloud — the music players load only when you press play. When you do, SoundCloud receives your IP address and may set its own cookies. See SoundCloud’s privacy policy and cookie policy.
  • Google Workspace hosts our email, so messages you send to a clavelofficial.com address are stored by Google. See Google’s privacy policy.

Links to Instagram, TikTok, SoundCloud and Apple Music take you to those services; their own privacy policies apply there.

We don’t sell or rent personal information, and we don’t use it for advertising.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies and uses no similar storage, so there is nothing to accept or decline. SoundCloud may set cookies only after you choose to play a track, as described above.

Do Not Track

This site doesn’t track visitors, on this site or across others, so it treats everyone the same whether or not your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal.

How long we keep things

Emails are kept as long as they’re needed to deal with what you asked about, and as part of normal business records for bookings.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing info@clavelofficial.com. We’ll answer within 30 days. If you’re in the EU or UK, you can also object to how your information is used and complain to your local data protection authority; the basis for using your email is replying to the message you sent.

Children

This site isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date at the top.
